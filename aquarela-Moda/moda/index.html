Questions, answered.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a production tool. Your real product photos stay the source: AI builds the scenes, models and backgrounds around them, and you approve every piece that shows your product.

Do you run our ads?

We make the ad creatives. Your team or your media buyer runs the campaigns, and ad spend isn’t included in any plan.

Who owns the files?

You do, once the month is paid. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

What counts as a round of changes?

One consolidated list of edits for a piece. A brand-new concept counts as a new piece.

What if we don’t use every piece?

Unused pieces roll over to the next month, once.

Can we start in January?

Yes. Founding Five brands sign by December 31 and pick a January start date.
